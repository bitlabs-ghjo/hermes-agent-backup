# Item-first government-support and R&D proposals

## Purpose

Use when a small technology company asks for several practical product ideas to
prepare for a future funding cycle. The deliverable is a ranked opportunity set,
not a list of subsidies. The following sources were inspected in September 2026;
they are historical evidence, not a standing shortlist or current eligibility.

## Evidence bank: what each source actually supports

- Manufacturing AI item specifications (2025):
  https://gnsquare.com/gditPortal/users/board/txtBoard/downloadFile.do?sn=66
  The retrieved PDF had 35 pages. Page 7 states growing industrial AS workloads,
  staffing limits and delayed responses. Pages 3–4 and 19 describe manual visual
  inspection and high-mix low-volume manufacturing. Pages 17–18 discuss
  equipment-level electricity forecasting and existing FEMS inputs. Use the
  problem statements, not the budget tables or performance targets as forecasts.
  Some tables contain inconsistent units/time references: inspect visually if
  those values become decision-critical.
- Follow-up AS/document implementation reporting:
  https://www.etnews.com/20251217000099
  Describes AS email identification, automotive quality-document search, and
  document structuring. This is reported implementation evidence, not an
  independent audit or proof of a new customer's willingness to buy. It does
  not directly prove repeated inspection-certificate/shipping-spec mismatches.
- Automotive manufacturing interview:
  https://v.daum.net/v/20260611085857743
  Describes beginning with standardizing and automating downtime records.
  This supports the value of a narrow intervention, not an assertion that the
  interviewed company still has the same unsolved problem.
- Ulsan process-safety document assistance:
  https://max.uipa.or.kr/info_02.html?query=view&id=6&page=1
  Describes JSA writing/review burden and specialist knowledge assistance.
  Do not transfer safety-workflow claims directly to all factory documentation,
  or imply that an assistant can approve safety-critical work.
- Ulsan energy/process case:
  https://max.uipa.or.kr/info_02.html?query=view&id=8&page=1
  Electricity savings from avoiding unnecessary operation appear as expected
  effects. Do not report them as measured savings or promise a saving percentage.

## Evaluation patterns by product class

| Product class | Baseline | Independent evaluation | Hard readiness gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| AS intake assistance | Existing ticket forms and rule-based extraction | Equipment identification, field errors, evidence validity, human correction and total processing time; split by customer/equipment | Lawful email use, equipment master, accountable reviewer |
| High-mix visual inspection | Lighting fixes, template matching, current inspection tool | Unseen part/lot/day; missed defects and false rejects, changeover time, label demand and latency | Stable capture setup and actual defect ground truth |
| Downtime-record assistance | Better codes, forms and spreadsheet summaries | Missing records, unclassified reasons and correction time | Agreed reason taxonomy and synchronized logs |
| Revision-aware document retrieval | Keyword search and generic RAG | Correct revision/page, obsolete citations, authorization breaches and appropriate abstention | Approved documents and a revision owner |
| Idle-energy analysis | Fixed schedules, threshold alarms and simple regression | Actionable alerts, false alarms and consumption normalized for production mix/environment | Metering, time alignment and implementation partner |
| Cross-document numerical checks | OCR, rules and spreadsheet validation | Critical mismatch misses, false alarms and review time | Real document pairs, approved specification and evidence of repeated pain |

Do not infer downtime reduction from better documentation alone. Do not infer
cash savings directly from minutes saved. For rare defects, absence of defect
examples prevents credible missed-defect validation. Numerical comparisons and
unit conversions should use deterministic code; AI may extract ambiguous inputs
but should not silently own acceptance decisions.

## Next-year notice classification: worked example

A next-year notice may already exist while ordinary company applications remain
unannounced. The inspected 2027 smart-factory operator notice explicitly says it
recruits operators and plans a later beneficiary call; it also permits schedule
changes:
https://www.bizinfo.go.kr/cmm/fms/fileDown.do?atchFileId=FILE_000000000773639&fileSn=0

Do not say 'no 2027 announcements exist' when only the desired beneficiary route
is unconfirmed. Distinguish operator selection, supplier-pool admission, and
purchaser applications in both the report and the eligibility matrix.

## Research and delivery checks

1. Use past research to locate original sources, then re-open load-bearing
   originals. Do not cite session memory as an official record.
2. Separate source facts, inferred product fit, and unverified current demand.
3. Count distinct items in the final artifact, not merely in the plan.
4. Use one parent citation ledger when combining parallel findings; attach
   verbatim evidence and verify references after adding the funding section.
5. A low automated citation-coverage score can reflect numerous clearly labeled
   planning proposals. Review external factual claims individually; do not add
   unrelated citations to invented estimates to raise a score.
6. Reuse an established report template rather than initiating a branding
   exercise for routine research. HTML parsing/link checks are not visual QA.
7. Keep research completion, artifact verification, approval, and delivery as
   separate states. Respect the active reporting destination and approval rules;
   never call a prepared attachment posted or received without verification.
