# Comparing regional expert-participation routes

## Reusable investigation pattern

1. Search the role as well as the institution: `전문상담위원`, `현장클리닉위원`, `평가위원`, `외부전문가`, `멘토 모집`, plus the region and year. Open the original notice and recruitment attachment; beneficiary recruitment is not expert recruitment.
2. Classify the audience and work before ranking. Field enterprise advice, project evaluation, startup mentoring, and youth/career counseling create different relationships. Maintain separate recommendations for strategic fit and time-sensitive eligibility checks.
3. Compare registration, qualification review, appointment, actual matching, and commercial permissions as separate stages. Record what was verified at each stage.
4. Compare summary-page rules against current operating guidelines. Employment years do not automatically establish consulting years; a founder title does not automatically prove a required corporate rank. Record exactly which evidence is required, and ask the operator about equivalent-expertise clauses rather than declaring eligibility.
5. Search for later additional calls and reform/cancellation notices before recommending an annual program. Retain closed or suspended routes as clearly dated preparation evidence, not current opportunities.
6. Deliver a compact comparison: institution; exact role; audience; status and cutoff; hard qualification/evidence gates; official contact; next action. Separate a historical reference appendix from the current shortlist. Do not claim an exhaustive regional inventory unless the scope was enumerated and reconciled.

## Retrieval and evidence hygiene

A successful public-search fallback used URL-encoded Korean queries at `https://search.naver.com/search.naver?query=...`, retrieving HTML with requests and parsing with BeautifulSoup. Use search results only to discover original URLs; fetch agency notices and attachments for substantive claims. This is an optional alternative, not a claim that other search tools are unavailable.

For parallel work, assign each child its own evidence-file prefix and let the parent own the final citation ledger. Return source URLs and supporting passages rather than merging child-local citation numbers. Verify actual files and original-source text before accepting child conclusions.
