# Two-Hour AX Workshop Blueprint

Use this as a worked pattern, then adapt examples, language, and sources to the audience. It was developed for a mixed cohort of SME manufacturers and small-business owners.

## Positioning

**Title pattern:** “The 30-Day AX Start for Owners: People Decide, AI Reduces Repetition.”

**Promise:** Participants leave with one prioritized task, a safe AI-assisted workflow, and a measured 30-day plan containing a 2-week prototype.

## Verified 120-Minute Agenda

| Minutes | Session | Method | Participant output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 8 | Opening diagnosis | Poll and business problem framing | Personal pain point |
| 15 | AX versus tool adoption | Executive mini-lecture | Decision criteria |
| 20 | Segment cases | Manufacturer and merchant comparisons | Candidate ideas |
| 15 | One common workflow demo | Messy notes to actions and review checklist | Prompt/workflow pattern |
| 10 | Break and question collection | Written questions | Question queue |
| 22 | Practice 1: choose the work | Individual scoring and peer review | One priority task |
| 18 | Practice 2: design the workflow | Canvas and prompt drafting | Workflow draft |
| 10 | Build the 30-day plan | KPI, owner, review, stop rule | Experiment card |
| 2 | Close | One-sentence commitment and evaluation | Declared next action |

Total: 120 minutes. Active design/practice/commitment time is 50 minutes, excluding the opening poll.

## Candidate-Task Scorecard

Score each item from 1 to 5:

- frequency
- current time burden
- output standardization
- data readiness
- ease of human verification
- business impact
- failure severity (reverse score)

Start with high-frequency, structured work whose mistakes are visible and reversible.

## Workflow Canvas

1. current task and business problem
2. input and lawful source
3. AI step: draft, summarize, classify, compare, or standardize
4. required output format
5. human reviewer and final decision
6. prohibited or de-identified data
7. quality checks
8. baseline and KPI
9. owner and review date
10. stop, revise, or expand threshold

## Manufacturing Micro-Cases

- Turn daily production-meeting notes into issues, owners, and due dates.
- Classify nonconformity or complaint narratives and draft root-cause questions.
- Compare RFQs/specifications and surface missing confirmations.
- Convert inspection records into preventive checklists and handover notes.
- Standardize or translate work-instruction drafts with mandatory shop-floor review.

Avoid presenting predictive maintenance or automated quality judgment as an easy first project.

## Small-Business Micro-Cases

- Categorize reviews and inquiries to prioritize service improvements.
- Convert product/menu facts into channel-specific descriptions.
- Draft repeated-inquiry responses with an owner-approved policy.
- Interpret spreadsheet/POS patterns and generate questions for validation.
- Turn promotion ideas into plans with audience, offer, channel, and metric.

Avoid autonomous pricing, compensation, or customer messaging without approval controls.

## Reusable Prompt Pattern

```text
Role: You assist with [business task].
Purpose: Produce [business outcome] from the input below.
Input: [dummy or de-identified data].
Procedure: Separate facts from inferences, identify missing data, and follow the requested format.
Output format: [table/list/draft].
Verification: Check [accuracy, omissions, numbers, prohibited language].
Constraint: Do not guess. Mark unknowns as “confirmation needed.”
Human decision: [named role] reviews and approves before use.
```

## 30-Day Experiment

- Week 1: measure baseline time, errors, rework, or waiting.
- Week 2: test the prototype on about ten dummy/de-identified samples.
- Week 3: refine the template and human-review procedure.
- Week 4: compare the same KPI and decide to stop, revise, or expand.

Choose only one or two KPIs.

## Specialist Reconciliation Pattern

Three perspectives commonly conflict:

- Research wants local relevance and warns against unsupported claims.
- Business development wants economic language and a follow-up path.
- Instructional design wants practice time, artifacts, and fallback delivery.

A sound resolution is a 70/30 common-versus-segment structure, one deep demo, a 2-week prototype inside a 30-day measurement window, and an optional fit-check occupying less than 10% of class time.
