# AI Crew Role Interview

Conduct in rounds of no more than five questions.

## Preflight — Portfolio fit

Before interviewing for another worker, answer:

1. Which existing workers own adjacent duties?
2. What recurring bottleneck or dropped handoff requires a new owner?
3. What unique output will this worker produce, and who consumes it?
4. Could this be a new skill or expanded charter for an existing worker instead of a separate profile?
5. Does the workload justify separate memory, credentials, and review overhead?

If the role is still distinct, define its upstream inputs and downstream handoff before Round 1.

## Round 1 — Organization and outcomes

1. What does the organization do, and what is its current stage?
2. What outcomes define success for this role?
3. What recurring deliverables must the worker produce?
4. Who is the direct manager and final decision-maker?
5. What is explicitly outside the role?

## Round 2 — Domain standards

1. Which systems or sources are authoritative?
2. What evidence/citation standard is required?
3. What format, depth, language, and cadence should outputs use?
4. Should work be request-only, scheduled, or proactively triggered?
5. What should happen when evidence is incomplete or sources conflict?

## Round 3 — Operations and authority

1. What may the worker do without approval?
2. Which writes, sends, submissions, purchases, or commitments require approval?
3. What must an approval request display?
4. Which information classes are confidential?
5. What is the escalation path for risk, missed deadlines, or conflicting instructions?

## Round 4 — Identity and collaboration

1. What is the worker’s name and title?
2. How should it address the executive, manager, peers, and itself?
3. What communication style should it use?
4. When should it disagree, and how should disagreement be structured?
5. Which other agents can assign work or receive reports?

## Final consistency check

Before drafting:

- Resolve typos in named systems instead of silently normalizing identifiers.
- Resolve combined-question answers that only address one sub-question.
- Check that cadence agrees with proactive/request-only policy.
- Check that tool permissions agree with the authority boundary.
- Check that direct reporting and approval routing are not contradictory.
