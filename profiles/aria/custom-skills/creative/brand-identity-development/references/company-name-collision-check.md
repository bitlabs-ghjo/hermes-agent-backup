# Company-name collision check

Use this reference during naming, logo, business-card, and public identity work. It is a lightweight risk screen, not legal clearance.

## Evidence sequence

1. Search the exact quoted name.
2. Repeat with capitalization, spacing, and common suffix variants.
3. Open or directly fetch the primary `.com` and category-relevant domains.
4. Record the page title, description, current use, and checked date.
5. Compare each active match on:
   - industry/category;
   - products or services;
   - target customers;
   - operating geography;
   - tagline and search-result language;
   - domain and social-handle overlap.
6. Search the appropriate official trademark registry separately.
7. Label registry results as provisional unless class, owner, status, and relevant jurisdiction were actually reviewed.

## Risk rubric

- **Low:** same string appears only in unrelated or inactive contexts; preferred domain/handles remain practical.
- **Moderate:** active same-name company exists in a different category or geography; search and referral confusion is plausible.
- **High:** active same-name company operates in the same or adjacent category, controls the obvious domain, or uses a similar promise.
- **Unresolved:** search evidence is partial, the registry is inaccessible, or ownership/status cannot be verified.

Do not downgrade risk solely because the other company is overseas. Search engines, domains, app stores, software buyers, and international customers cross jurisdictions.

## Report pattern

```markdown
## Name and domain risk

**Finding:** [Low / Moderate / High / Unresolved]

- [name/domain] — [verified current use], checked [date], source: [URL]
- [name/domain] — [verified current use], checked [date], source: [URL]

**Likely impact:** [search/domain/referral/market confusion]

**Verified:** [live websites/search results actually inspected]
**Not verified:** [trademark class/status, legal-name registry, handles]

**Recommendation:** [continue / add modifier / test alternatives / obtain trademark review] before [print, launch, or distribution].
```

## Boundaries

- A search result is evidence of use, not proof of trademark ownership.
- An available domain is not proof that a name is safe.
- A registered local business name is not proof that international branding is safe.
- Do not claim legal clearance unless an authorized professional or definitive registry review supports it.
- Keep collision findings prominent enough to affect the design decision; do not bury them in a final disclaimer.
